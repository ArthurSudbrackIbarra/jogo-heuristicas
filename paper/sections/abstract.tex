\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Introduction}: Teaching Nielsen's usability heuristics traditionally relies on reading abstract definitions, which novices struggle to internalize.
\textbf{Objective}: To support the teaching of Nielsen's heuristics through paired interactive scenarios.
\textbf{Methodology}: Design and evaluation of a web-based educational game with paired violating and compliant interfaces, narration, and gamification; open-ended responses of 31 participants were analyzed via qualitative content analysis.
\textbf{Results}: Participants named the paired bad/good contrast and the interactive scenarios as the main support mechanisms; narration helped most participants and frustrated some, while gamification worked as engagement support rather than as a learning driver.
\end{abstract}
 
\keywords{Nielsen's usability heuristics, HCI education, educational game, game-based learning}
