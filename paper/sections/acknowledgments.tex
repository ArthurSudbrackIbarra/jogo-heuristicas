\section*{Acknowledgments}

The authors thank the participants who took the time to play the game and to share their experience through the questionnaire.

We disclose the use of generative AI tools in the preparation of this paper. Claude (Anthropic) was used to enhance the writing of the text, suggesting synonyms and connectives to improve clarity and fluency. Grammarly was used to review grammar and spelling in the English text. A machine-translation tool was used to translate selected Portuguese-language references and excerpts into English. The ideas, arguments, technical claims, artefact design, methodology, results, and conclusions presented in this paper are the authors' own.
